## 1. What Lumina is

Lumina is an independent AI study companion for students who use Canvas. It reads your courses and assignments from Canvas (with an access token you provide) and uses an AI tutor to help you study. It is built and run by Anvith Yalamanchili, a student.

Lumina is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by** Instructure (Canvas), any school, the International Baccalaureate Organization, College Board, or Google. Names such as "IB" or "AP" are used only to describe the courses and exam formats the tutor can help with.

You must be **at least 13** and have a Canvas account at a school Lumina supports. If you're under 18, you should have a parent or guardian's permission and your school's okay. Lumina does not currently verify age, so you are responsible for meeting this requirement.

## 3. Your account and your Canvas key

You're responsible for the accuracy of what you provide and for keeping your password secure, and for everything that happens under your account. Your Canvas access token lets Lumina **read** your Canvas data. Lumina never writes to Canvas. You can cut that access off at any time by deleting the token in Canvas (Account → Settings), and you can delete your Lumina account yourself in **Settings → Account → Danger zone**.

## 4. Study honestly

Lumina is built to **guide you to the answer, not to hand it over**: for problem-solving questions the tutor gives hints and leading questions rather than finished work. But you remain responsible for your own work. Follow your school's and exam board's rules on AI assistance, and don't submit work as your own if those rules say it must be entirely yours. Lumina is not responsible for how you use its output.

## 5. AI answers and Canvas data can be wrong

The tutor is an AI model and can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Check important facts against your textbook or teacher. Assignment and exam dates come from your last Canvas sync, so they may be stale. **Always confirm deadlines in Canvas.** Lumina is a study aid, not a substitute for your teachers.

Because answers are produced by a third-party AI provider, **don't enter other people's personal details, passwords, health information, or anything you wouldn't want a third party to see.** See the Privacy Policy for what is sent to the provider.
